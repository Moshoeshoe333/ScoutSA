Match-day control

Select the fixture and record operational state. A status change does not certify that the match occurred.

Match officials

Assign a synthetic official role to this fixture. The prototype does not verify qualifications or appointments.

Team lineups

Lineup membership is fixture-specific. Only the two synthetic adult sample participations are available.

Player appearances & minutes

A derived view of the selected fixture. Starters begin at minute 0; substitutes begin at their recorded entry minute; unused players receive zero minutes. A substitution's recorded minute determines the outgoing player's end minute. These are demo calculations, not official match records.

Derivation: Lineup ID + fixture-specific substitution record(s). No appearance is inferred from goals or other match events. Missing or inconsistent timing is not silently converted into a verified statistic.

Substitutions

A substitution links two players from the same team in the same fixture. Both must already have lineup records.

Match events

Events require a participating player and a source record. The event log does not make the source authoritative.

Match report

One report per fixture in this demo. The report has a source, author label and review state; it is not a certified referee record.

Evidence and relationship ledger

Every event and report source becomes a source record with a stable ID, type and originating record.

Provenance fields: source ID, source label, source type, linked fixture, linked record ID and review state. This demo does not store uploaded files or cryptographic fingerprints.
