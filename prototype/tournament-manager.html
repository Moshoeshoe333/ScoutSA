Event setup

Create the tournament identity and one edition. The organizer is a separate record.

Feature permission is distinct from organizer identity verification and player/media permissions.
Organizer contact details are intentionally not collected in this demo. A future contact workflow must record public source, date checked, representative authority and consent to contact.

Competition divisions

Divisions belong to a tournament edition and define the competition stream.

Team registration & coach assignment

A team is a stable identity. Its tournament entry and coach assignment are separate event-scoped records.

Player participation

Participation links a player identity to one tournament team entry. This demo only allows clearly synthetic adult sample records.

Use a label such as DEMO-PLAYER-003, never a real person's name.

Fixture creation

A fixture must use two different team entries from the same division.

Match result

Results attach to an existing fixture. Corrections should be handled as auditable revisions in a production system.

Match statistics

Every stat observation is scoped to a player participation and fixture, with a definition, unit, source and review state.

A statistic is an observation, not a scouting verdict. Missing data is not zero; metric definitions and source methods must remain visible.

Competition eligibility review

A separate, evidence-aware review linked to a player participation and its division. It records a review state; it does not certify age, identity or eligibility.

Safeguarding boundary: this synthetic workflow does not collect dates of birth, identity documents or real minor data. Evidence is represented by demo references only. “Eligible for human review” means only that a reviewer may consider the record under the cited competition rule; it is not an approval or selection.
Eligibility reviews are event- and participation-scoped. A change to the division rule, evidence or participation requires a new review record in a production design; this demo does not provide immutable history.
