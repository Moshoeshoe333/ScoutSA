# ScoutSA Opportunity Validation Scenario Pack — Proposal

**Status:** Research/validation proposal — **NOT RATIFIED**  
**Branch:** `prototype/internal-demo`  
**As of:** 2026-11-13  
**Authority:** None. These scenarios are synthetic test cases, not real reports, legal conclusions, safeguarding certifications, eligibility rules or implementation authorization.

## 1. Purpose and use

This pack stress-tests the proposed [Opportunity Evidence Acceptance Matrix](OPPORTUNITY-EVIDENCE-ACCEPTANCE-MATRIX.md) and [Opportunity Requirement Declaration](OPPORTUNITY-REQUIREMENT-DECLARATION.md) against plausible grassroots football situations relevant to the Vaal Triangle and wider South Africa.

The purpose is not to prove the proposals correct. It is to find where they:
- exclude legitimate community participation without a defensible reason;
- accept weak or self-interested claims too easily;
- confuse an activity, invitation, registration, scouting observation and placement;
- expose children or families to unsafe contact, fees, travel or data collection;
- leave people unable to challenge an incorrect decision.

All people, organizations, venues, messages, amounts and records in the cases below are fictional. They must not be interpreted as allegations about any real club, academy, coach, scout or individual.

**Use rule:** reviewers assess the exact claim and proposed stage, record uncertainty, identify the authority boundary, and explain the least harmful safe next step. They must not invent evidence to make a case pass.

## 2. Common decision vocabulary

These are proposed review outcomes for the exercise, not implemented engine states:

| Outcome | Meaning in this exercise |
|---|---|
| **STOP — safeguarding** | Do not progress the relevant contact or activity while a material safeguarding risk is unresolved. Escalate through an appropriate responsible route. |
| **HOLD — evidence/authority** | Do not approve the specific claim or release stage until the missing authority, evidence or clarification is independently reviewed. |
| **REVIEW — bounded** | The activity may be considered for the stated limited purpose only after required safeguards and authority checks; do not imply broader verification. |
| **NOT ESTABLISHED** | Available evidence does not establish this proposition. It is not proof that the person or claim is false. |
| **DISPUTED** | Preserve the original assertion and the counter-assertion; do not silently overwrite either. |
| **OUT OF SCOPE** | The case requires policy, legal, safeguarding or operational decisions beyond this proposal. |

A “review” is not permission to publish, invite, contact, collect data or run an event. Each release stage needs its own explicit authorization.

## 3. Scenario cards

### V-01 — Informal community coach, open session, no club registration

**Situation:** A coach runs a free Saturday football session in a Vaal community field. The group has no formal club registration. The coach can identify the session location and describe their first-hand role. The coach asks ScoutSA to list an open development session.

**Claims to separate:**
- The session exists and is scheduled.
- The submitter is responsible for the session.
- Attendance is open and free.
- The session is safe and appropriately supervised.

**Evidence questions:** Is there a reliable venue/date confirmation? Who controls the venue? What first-hand basis supports the coach's role? Who is the accountable adult on site? What is the arrival/departure and incident process?

**Candidate outcome:** **REVIEW — bounded** for the existence and description of a community activity if the source and authority are independently checked. No formal club registration should be demanded merely to establish that a community session exists. Safeguarding and venue conditions remain separate blockers.

**Fairness trap:** Rejecting the session solely because it lacks formal club affiliation would confuse formal registration with community activity.

**Safety stop:** If no accountable adult, safe venue arrangement or child-appropriate supervision can be established, do not progress a child-facing listing or attendance workflow.

**Reviewer challenge:** What exact claim would a coach's first-hand attestation establish, and what would it not establish?

### V-02 — Academy assessment with a vague “verified player” requirement

**Situation:** A fictional academy advertises an assessment and says only “verified players accepted”. It provides no definition, source, competition rule or reason for this requirement. A player has community experience but no formal club record.

**Claims to separate:** The assessment exists; the academy has authority to offer it; the “verified” condition is genuinely mandatory; the player meets that condition.

**Evidence questions:** What does “verified” mean? Who set the criterion? Is it required by a named competition or simply the academy's preference? Is there a proportionate alternative way to assess community-experienced players?

**Candidate outcome:** **HOLD** the ambiguous eligibility criterion and the representation that it is mandatory. The assessment itself is not automatically disproven. Ask the owner to rewrite the criterion as a specific, relevant proposition and identify its authority.

**Fairness trap:** Treating an undefined badge or status as a universal gate to opportunity.

**Reviewer challenge:** Could the academy assess football ability without first requiring formal affiliation? If not, what is the exact operational or competition rule?

### V-03 — Real organization identity, unproven representative authority

**Situation:** A submitter uses the name and logo of a real-seeming academy. A public page confirms that the academy exists, but the submitter provides no evidence they are authorized to advertise this specific assessment.

**Claims to separate:** The organization exists; the submitter represents it; the submitter may make this offer; the offer details are accurate.

**Evidence questions:** Is there a trusted organization-controlled confirmation channel? Can an authorized representative confirm the person's role and this particular event? Does the confirmation cover dates, fees, venue and participant cohort?

**Candidate outcome:** **HOLD** representative authority and the offer. The existence of an organization must not be treated as proof that the submitter speaks for it.

**Safety stop:** Do not route players or guardians to private contact details supplied only by the unverified submitter.

**Reviewer challenge:** What independent source can verify the authority without relying on the same logo, profile or message?

### V-04 — Expired coach mandate / former employee

**Situation:** A coach previously worked with a club and submits a new opportunity using an old club letter or email. The mandate appears to have expired, or the named contact is no longer in the role.

**Claims to separate:** Historical affiliation; current employment/role; current authority; current offer.

**Candidate outcome:** **HOLD** current authority. Historical proof may establish a past relationship only. Request fresh confirmation through an organization-controlled channel.

**Fairness trap:** Treating a formerly valid document as current authority.

**Reviewer challenge:** What is the relevant effective period, and what event should trigger re-review?

### V-05 — “Free trial” followed by a pay-to-be-seen fee

**Situation:** An advert says an assessment is free. After a player expresses interest, an intermediary asks for a fee to “secure visibility” or “guarantee scout attention”. The payment recipient and refund terms are unclear.

**Claims to separate:** The advertised activity is free; a fee is required; who receives it; what service is promised; whether the intermediary is authorized.

**Candidate outcome:** **STOP** the payment/contact progression pending investigation and **HOLD** the opportunity representation. Preserve the advert and the changed request. Do not label the payment legitimate because the owner calls it an administration fee.

**Safety stop:** Do not encourage payment or sharing of identity documents. Escalate through a safe adult/guardian route where a minor is involved.

**Reviewer challenge:** Is any fee actually required to participate, be seen, be considered or be selected? What does the recipient promise in writing?

### V-06 — Venue and date are only “being arranged”

**Situation:** A social post announces a trial at a named stadium. The submitter later admits that no venue booking or date confirmation exists.

**Claims to separate:** An event is proposed; a venue is booked; the date is confirmed; access and supervision are arranged.

**Candidate outcome:** **HOLD** any representation that the event is confirmed. A proposed event may be described as unconfirmed only in an authorized, non-player-facing planning context; do not publish it as a confirmed opportunity.

**Fairness trap:** Marketing language creates false certainty and may cause families to spend money on transport.

**Reviewer challenge:** What source confirms the venue, booking period, access permission and responsible on-site party?

### V-07 — Criteria or fees change after interest is received

**Situation:** An owner initially accepts ages 14–17 and says participation is free. After players express interest, the owner changes the age band and introduces a fee.

**Claims to separate:** Original terms; revised terms; who authorized the change; who has already relied on the original terms.

**Candidate outcome:** **HOLD** the changed offer and require a new version and independent review. Preserve the prior declaration. Participants must receive clear notice and a fair withdrawal/correction route before any further action.

**Safety stop:** Do not use a changed age range to bypass guardian or child safeguards.

**Reviewer challenge:** Which applicants are affected, what costs have they incurred, and how can they withdraw without penalty?

### V-08 — Coach attendance attestation misused as league registration

**Situation:** A community coach truthfully confirms that a player attended training for six months. An opportunity owner treats that statement as proof that the player is formally registered with a league for the current season.

**Claims to separate:** Attendance; coach-player relationship; formal club affiliation; current league registration.

**Candidate outcome:** Accept the coach statement only for the first-hand attendance proposition, if the source and scope are credible. **NOT ESTABLISHED** for formal registration unless the authoritative current registration source is supplied and reviewed.

**Fairness trap:** Overextending a valid source beyond its competence.

**Reviewer challenge:** Does the evidence answer the exact question, for the correct person, organization, competition and season?

### V-09 — Genuine registration record from a previous season

**Situation:** A player provides a genuine registration confirmation, but it relates to the prior season. The current opportunity requires current-season eligibility.

**Candidate outcome:** The record may establish historical registration for the period it covers. **NOT ESTABLISHED** for current-season eligibility. Do not call the old record fraudulent or infer that the player is ineligible for every activity.

**Reviewer challenge:** What period does the record cover, who issued it, and what current source can resolve the gap?

### V-10 — Football eligibility but no safe supervision or transport plan

**Situation:** An opportunity appears legitimate and its football eligibility criteria are clear. However, the owner cannot identify the responsible safeguarding lead, explain supervision, or describe safe arrival/departure and transport arrangements for minors.

**Candidate outcome:** **STOP — safeguarding** for child-facing progression. Football eligibility does not compensate for unsafe operating conditions.

**Reviewer challenge:** Who is accountable on site? Who supervises? Who receives and hands over the child? What happens during an incident or cancellation?

**Boundary:** This scenario does not prescribe a statutory supervision ratio or declare legal compliance. Those require qualified, context-specific review.

### V-11 — Intermediary privately contacts a minor and requests ID/payment

**Situation:** A person claiming to be a scout contacts a minor's private social account, asks for identity documents and requests money to arrange a trial.

**Candidate outcome:** **STOP — safeguarding** and preserve available evidence safely. Do not continue the private exchange, forward the minor's details, or ask the child to investigate the adult. Use an appropriate guardian/responsible-adult route and qualified reporting/support channels.

**Reviewer challenge:** Is the intermediary authorized? Is any relevant agent licensing claim independently verifiable? Who is the safe adult contact? What information must be protected?

**Boundary:** A claimed scout title, badge or FIFA-related reference is not proof of licensing or authority.

### V-12 — Player disputes a rejection and asks for correction

**Situation:** A player says a reviewer rejected them because they were “not registered”. The player believes the criterion was not required for the advertised open community session. The owner wants to replace the original review note with “player did not qualify”.

**Candidate outcome:** **DISPUTED**. Preserve the original criterion, evidence, decision and player/guardian counter-claim. Have an independent reviewer test whether the requirement was relevant and disclosed. Correct the record through a traceable version, not silent overwrite.

**Fairness trap:** A neutral “not established” state is turned into a character judgment or a permanent negative label.

**Reviewer challenge:** What was the exact offer, what was disclosed at the time, who set the criterion, and what evidence supports its relevance?

### V-13 — Scholarship requests excessive personal information too early

**Situation:** A purported scholarship asks for identity documents, school records, household income and health information before explaining the funder, purpose, recipients, retention period or selection process.

**Candidate outcome:** **HOLD** the data request and any player-data processing. Ask the owner to identify the accountable funder, exact purpose for each data item, necessity, recipients, retention, security, correction and deletion process. Consider whether initial eligibility can be assessed with less information.

**Safety stop:** Do not upload sensitive records to an unreviewed opportunity or share a minor's documents through an informal personal account.

**Boundary:** This pack does not determine a lawful basis or certify POPIA compliance. Qualified privacy/legal review is required before real processing.

### V-14 — Two reviewers rely on one unverified source

**Situation:** Two reviewers independently mark an organization claim as confirmed, but both rely on the same forwarded screenshot from an unknown sender.

**Candidate outcome:** **HOLD** the claim. Two reviewers are not independent corroboration when the underlying source is shared and unverified. Record the common origin and seek a source with authority for the exact proposition.

**Reviewer challenge:** Are the sources genuinely independent, or are they copies/derivatives of one claim?

## 4. Cross-case adversarial checks

Every scenario should be reviewed against these questions:

1. **Proposition:** What exact statement is being established? Is it narrower than the marketing language?
2. **Authority:** Is the source competent to establish this particular statement?
3. **Identity binding:** Does the evidence refer to the correct person, organization, representative, event, season and location?
4. **Freshness:** Is it current for the relevant period? What expires or changes?
5. **Independence:** Do corroborating sources have genuinely separate origins?
6. **Proportionality:** Is the evidence burden necessary for this stage, or does it exclude community participants without a relevant reason?
7. **Uncertainty:** Is missing proof recorded neutrally, without converting it into an accusation?
8. **Safeguarding:** Could the proposed next step expose a child to private contact, unsafe travel, unsupervised activity, coercion or inappropriate disclosure?
9. **Privacy:** Is each data item necessary now, for a stated purpose and a defined recipient?
10. **Stage:** Does the evidence justify only discovery, interest, invitation, attendance, selection or formal registration? Never silently promote one stage to another.
11. **Dispute:** Can a player or guardian challenge the claim and preserve a counter-record?
12. **Change:** Will a material change trigger a new version and review?

## 5. Suggested reviewer worksheet

For each case, record:

| Field | Reviewer entry |
|---|---|
| Scenario ID | V-01 to V-14 |
| Exact claim | One proposition per line |
| Requested stage | Discovery / interest / application / invitation / attendance / selection / registration / placement |
| Source and origin | Who created it, how obtained, and whether first-hand |
| Source authority | Why this source can establish this proposition |
| Scope and freshness | Person/entity, season, event, date and expiry |
| Missing or contradictory evidence | Explicitly list; do not infer |
| Safeguarding stop | Risk, responsible role, safe next step |
| Privacy concern | Data item, purpose, recipient and minimization alternative |
| Candidate outcome | STOP / HOLD / REVIEW — bounded / NOT ESTABLISHED / DISPUTED / OUT OF SCOPE |
| Decision owner | Role authorized to decide; conflict disclosed |
| Participant explanation | Plain-language reason and correction route |
| Re-review trigger | New evidence, expiry, changed terms or incident |

Do not convert worksheet outcomes into a numeric trust score. Do not average a safeguarding stop against positive evidence. A single unresolved material safety issue remains a stop for the affected child-facing step.

## 6. Validation method and exit criteria

A future facilitated validation session should include distinct perspectives, where available:
- community coach or grassroots organizer;
- player voice appropriate to age and safeguarded participation;
- parent/guardian;
- academy or club representative;
- competition/league actor where registration rules are invoked;
- independent safeguarding practitioner;
- South African privacy/legal reviewer where personal data or child-related obligations arise.

Do not ask participants to provide real minors' identities, contact details, private messages or documents for this exercise. Use the fictional cases and synthetic evidence only. Do not present the exercise as legal advice or as proof of stakeholder endorsement.

For each scenario, capture:
- whether reviewers agree on the proposition and stage;
- which source they consider authoritative and why;
- where they disagree, and what rule or evidence would resolve it;
- false acceptance risk and false rejection risk;
- participant harm if the decision is wrong;
- evidence burden and access/fairness effect;
- safeguarding and privacy stop conditions;
- unresolved legal, operational or policy questions.

**Proposed exit criteria before ratification:**
1. Every scenario has a written, reasoned disposition and named unresolved questions.
2. Reviewers distinguish community activity from formal affiliation and do not impose formal registration universally.
3. No reviewer promotes attendance, observation or invitation into selection or placement without separate evidence.
4. No unsafe child-facing step is allowed to proceed because an opportunity otherwise looks legitimate.
5. Fees, intermediaries, authority, changes and disputes are traceable and challengeable.
6. Missing evidence is described neutrally and proportionately.
7. Reviewers can explain the decision to a player/guardian in plain language.
8. Relevant legal, privacy, safeguarding and competition questions are explicitly referred for qualified review.
9. Disagreements and minority views are preserved; consensus is not manufactured.
10. Governance decides whether any proposed change is warranted. Passing this pack does not itself ratify a policy or authorize implementation.

## 7. Known limitations and open questions

- These cases are hypothetical; they do not establish the prevalence of any practice in the Vaal or South Africa.
- They do not define statutory requirements, competition rules, agent licensing checks, supervision ratios, legal bases or retention periods.
- The pack does not verify real organizations, coaches, scouts, academies, events or venues.
- It does not define an operational incident-response service or promise that ScoutSA can investigate reports.
- It does not settle who has final authority to approve or suspend an opportunity.
- It does not establish that current prototype rules are fair, legally sufficient or ready for real-world use.

## 8. Gate boundary

**NOT RATIFIED. No implementation is authorized.**

This pack does not change the review engine, tests for policy behavior, Constitution, Domain Model, Relationship Model, schema boundary, database, RLS, API, authentication, UI, identity processing, consent handling, publication permissions, deployment or pilot authority.

Use it only as a structured research/validation proposal. Any eventual policy change must return through the established governance and ratification process. Until then, existing mainline holds and prototype safeguards remain unchanged.

**Principle:** A safe system must know when not to proceed, and a fair system must know when not to exclude.
