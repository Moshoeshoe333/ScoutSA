# ScoutSA product decision gate — thin MVP, redesign, or pause

**Status:** Decision framework recorded; no path is currently validated by external stakeholder evidence.
**Applies to:** Product scope and pilot direction. It does not authorize production implementation, personal-data processing, public listings, invitations, or release.

## Decision paths

### A — Narrow and build a thin MVP
**Trigger:** Feedback from relevant players, guardians, coaches and academies confirms that the core problem and proposed workflow are valuable and workable.

**Thin scope:** Player Passport foundation; guardian authority and purpose-specific consent; one controlled discovery/request path. Exclude broad social features, public player search, automated ranking, open contact, payments and complex organization tooling.

**Pilot target:** A usable, tightly controlled Vaal pilot within 6–10 weeks, but only after the required safeguarding, legal, consent, access-control, operational and technical release gates are satisfied. The timeframe is a planning hypothesis, not a commitment or evidence of feasibility.

### B — Redesign key parts
**Trigger:** Feedback identifies material friction or invalid assumptions in consent, readiness, verification, opportunity access, stakeholder roles or the discovery/request flow.

**Response:** Change only the domain concepts and workflows that the evidence requires. Preserve stable invariants. Record the source, affected claim, contradiction, decision and downstream impact. Re-test the revised thin surface before expanding scope.

### C — Pause or pivot
**Trigger:** Evidence supports the underlying problem but indicates the current product model is too heavy, mistimed, unsafe, commercially weak or aimed at the wrong stakeholder.

**Response:** Stop feature expansion. Preserve evidence and lessons, identify the more appropriate problem-owner or intervention, and decide whether to pause, reframe or discontinue.

## Evidence required before choosing

- Separate feedback from players, guardians, coaches, academies and relevant safeguarding/legal practitioners; do not treat one stakeholder group as representative of all.
- Record who was consulted, their role, method, date, exact claim, evidence strength, limitations and any dissent.
- Test whether users understand the distinction between football readiness and practical opportunity access.
- Test whether guardian authority, the player's voice, consent by purpose, withdrawal and disclosure limits are understandable and workable.
- Test whether organizations can establish identity and representative authority without being given excessive player data.
- Test whether a real opportunity can be independently verified and safely reached.
- Record negative evidence, non-response and unresolved questions. Silence is not validation.

## Current position

**No path selected.** The internal synthetic prototype can test interface logic, not stakeholder demand or legal/safeguarding adequacy. The lack of external confirmation means Path A remains conditional. If research exposes material workflow friction, take B. If it shows the approach is wrong or premature, take C.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- No production player data, minors' personal data, guardian documents or real consent in the static demo.
- No public minor profiles, direct private contact with minors, or invitation/publication workflow.
- No claims of POPIA compliance, verified organizations, secure access, RLS or live integration without specific evidence.
- Documentation, fixtures and UI pages are not implementation or release approval.
- A 6–10 week target cannot override release gates.
