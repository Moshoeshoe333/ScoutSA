# ScoutSA Opportunity-Specific Evidence Acceptance — Decision Aid

**Status:** Research/design proposal — **NOT RATIFIED**  
**Branch:** `prototype/internal-demo`  
**As of:** 2026-11-13  
**Authority:** None. This matrix does not amend the Constitution, Domain Model, Relationship Model, schema boundary, review-engine contract, or implementation permissions.

## 1. Decision question

When a football opportunity has a requirement, what evidence is appropriate to establish that exact requirement without turning formal registration into a universal gate for grassroots participation?

This matrix applies the proposed evidence classes in [the Affiliation Evidence Model](AFFILIATION-EVIDENCE-MODEL-PROPOSAL.md). It is a discussion aid. It is not an approved policy, eligibility standard, safeguarding procedure, or instruction to change the prototype engine.

## 2. Non-negotiable distinctions

1. **Opportunity access is not affiliation verification.** A player may be allowed to express interest or attend an open community session without first proving formal club membership.
2. **Affiliation is not ability.** A roster record, coach attestation, or player report says nothing by itself about talent, readiness, selection likelihood, or performance.
3. **Eligibility is not safeguarding clearance.** Meeting a football requirement never bypasses age, guardian, consent, identity, safeguarding, or safe-contact controls.
4. **A source proves only its own bounded proposition.** A coach may attest to sessions they personally supervised; that does not make the coach an authorized league registrar.
5. **No universal evidence ladder.** Evidence acceptance is tied to a defined proposition and opportunity, not a player's worth or a composite trust score.
6. **Missing evidence is not a negative fact.** Use “not established” or “insufficient evidence”; do not infer that a player never participated.

## 3. Opportunity evidence matrix — candidate defaults

The entries below are candidate defaults to challenge with grassroots coaches, academy recruiters, school sport coordinators, LFA/SAFA representatives, players and guardians. “May support” never means “automatically passes”.

| Opportunity / decision | Exact proposition to establish | Player-reported experience | Community-coach attestation | Formal organization record | Candidate minimum handling |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Open community development session | Player wishes to participate and meets the session's stated practical entry conditions | May support self-declared interest and relevant history; do not present as verified affiliation | May support prior participation with that coach if first-hand and scoped | Usually unnecessary unless the session itself has a lawful, clearly stated requirement | Do not demand formal club membership merely to express interest in an open session. Apply separate age, guardian, consent and safeguarding gates. |
| Coach-led recurring community programme | Player attended or participates in a named programme during a stated period | Preserve as the player's account; insufficient alone for an independently corroborated attendance claim | May support attendance/coaching relationship the coach personally observed; verify the attestor's identity and bounded role | May support programme enrolment if the organization and record are independently checked | Attestation may be proportionate for the coach's own sessions. Record dates, scope, limitations and conflicts; do not convert this into formal club registration. |
| Academy assessment / trial invitation | Player meets the academy's published, opportunity-specific prerequisites | May provide context and self-declared playing history; not proof of a required registration status | May provide bounded context about training or observed participation | Required only where the academy's stated criterion genuinely requires a formal status (for example a specific league registration); authenticate source and authority | Academy must state each prerequisite before review. Separate selection criteria from affiliation and safeguarding. Never invent a blanket formal-membership prerequisite. |
| Formal club membership / registration | Player is recorded as registered or formally affiliated with a named club for a defined period | Can preserve the player's account or raise a dispute; cannot independently establish formal registration | May support a coach's first-hand participation claim, but not formal registration unless that coach's authority for the exact registration action is independently established | Normally required from an authoritative club/league registration source, with period and status checked | Use the relevant registration authority and current/season-specific record. A coach's title, club logo, screenshot or old roster is not sufficient authentication by itself. |
| League eligibility / competition registration | Player satisfies the named competition's registration and eligibility rules | Can identify a possible mismatch or dispute; does not prove league registration | Can explain participation history; does not substitute for the competition authority's record | Required from the competent league/competition registration source for the relevant season and rule | The competition owner must identify the controlling rule and authoritative source. If not verified, record “not established”; do not imply the player lacks ability. |
| Scouting observation / talent-identification review | A scout or reviewer observed specified football actions at a stated event/time | May add player-supplied context, clearly labelled | May add a separate coach observation if first-hand and independently attributable | Affiliation records are not proof of observed performance; may be relevant only to a separately stated eligibility requirement | Performance observations require their own provenance, observer authority, date and context. Do not use affiliation class as a talent grade or selection proxy. |
| Scholarship / funded development opportunity | Player meets the published programme criteria (which may include need, geography, age, school or football participation) | May support self-declared context; sensitive claims require separate careful verification | May support a bounded football-participation claim | Required only for criteria that specifically concern formal enrolment or registration | Each criterion needs its own source and proportionate verification method. Do not let an affiliation record silently prove financial need, school status or guardian authority. |

## 4. Proposed acceptance rules

These are candidate rules for governance review, not ratified requirements.

### Rule A — define the proposition first

Every opportunity must state what it needs to establish in plain language. Examples:
- “The player attended Coach X's community sessions between March and June.”
- “The player is registered with Club Y for the 2026 season.”
- “The player meets the published age band for this assessment.”

Avoid vague requirements such as “verified player” or “trusted affiliation”. They collapse separate questions and invite arbitrary gatekeeping.

### Rule B — choose the source that has authority for that proposition

- A player is authoritative about what they are reporting as their own account, but that account is not independent corroboration.
- A coach can attest to first-hand actions and observations within their actual knowledge and role.
- A club or league record can support formal registration only when the issuing source and the representative's authority for that record are checked.
- A source's authority is scoped. Identity verification does not itself prove authority; organization verification does not itself prove representative authority.

### Rule C — independent corroboration is claim-specific

Do not require two sources for every low-risk self-reported fact by default; that can exclude players who have no paperwork. Do require stronger, independent evidence where the consequence or rule demands it, especially for formal registration, competition eligibility, disputed claims or high-impact decisions.

Two people repeating the same source are not independent. A coach and player repeating a shared unverified story are not automatically two independent witnesses. Record the relationship between sources and any shared origin.

### Rule D — preserve disputes without silently overwriting history

A player may disagree with an organization record; an organization may dispute a player report. Keep the original assertion, the counter-assertion, evidence references, decision scope, reviewer, time and appeal route. A dispute is not itself proof that either party is dishonest.

### Rule E — separate access stages

The following are different decisions and must not be collapsed:
- expressing interest;
- being invited to an open session;
- being considered for an assessment;
- meeting a published eligibility requirement;
- being selected;
- being registered;
- receiving an offer or placement.

Evidence appropriate for one stage may be insufficient for another. A low-friction discovery or interest path should not imply selection, eligibility, verified affiliation or guaranteed opportunity.

## 5. Candidate minimum-evidence profiles

| Claim type | Candidate minimum evidence | What the decision may say | What it must not say |
|---|---|---|---|
| Player's own football history | Attributable player assertion with period and source label | “Player-reported; not independently corroborated” | “Verified club member” |
| Coach's own training relationship | Identified coach; first-hand role and period; bounded observation; conflict/limitation recorded | “Coach-attested for these sessions/period” | “Academy-verified” unless authority is separately proven |
| Formal club registration | Authoritative club/league record; issuer and representative authority checked; season/status/period matched | “Registration corroborated for the stated period and source” | “Currently registered” outside the record's validity |
| Competition eligibility | Controlling competition rule plus authoritative current-season registration/eligibility evidence | “Requirement established for this competition and season” | “Eligible for all competitions” |
| Observed performance | Attributable observer, event/date, observation scope and method | “Observed at this event under these conditions” | “Guaranteed talent”, “trial ready”, or a broad quality claim unsupported by the observation |

## 6. Decision authority and separation of duties

A future approved policy should identify, for each opportunity:
- **Opportunity owner:** defines and publishes the actual requirement and is accountable for its accuracy.
- **Evidence submitter:** provides a claim or record and discloses their relationship to the player/organization.
- **Source/authority reviewer:** checks source identity, authority, provenance and scope.
- **Eligibility decision-maker:** decides only the published eligibility proposition.
- **Safeguarding reviewer:** independently handles safeguarding and safe-contact prerequisites; eligibility cannot overrule this role.
- **Appeal/dispute reviewer:** reviews material challenges without silently editing the original evidence.

One person may hold multiple roles only where a ratified policy allows it and conflicts are declared and controlled. The prototype does not yet define these permissions.

## 7. Required “not established” behavior

Use a neutral result when:
- the source cannot be authenticated;
- the source is not authorized for the claim;
- the record period does not match the requirement;
- evidence is incomplete or contradictory;
- only a player report exists for a formal registration requirement;
- a coach can attest to training but not institutional registration.

“Not established” means the evidence does not establish the proposition under the applicable rule. It does not mean “false”, “not talented”, “unsafe”, “not worthy”, or “not a real player”.

## 8. Adversarial scenarios to test before ratification

1. A talented player has trained informally for years and has never been on a formal club roster.
2. A community coach has first-hand knowledge but no registered organization or formal coaching certificate.
3. A coach uses an academy's name and badge but has no proven authority to represent it.
4. A club produces a genuine roster from a previous season.
5. A league database says registered; the player says the record is wrong or outdated.
6. An academy uses “verified affiliation” as an unexplained blanket prerequisite for an open assessment.
7. A scout observes a player at one event but has no basis to claim current club membership.
8. Two attestations trace back to the same original unverified source.
9. A minor is eligible on football criteria but the guardian/consent/safeguarding route is incomplete.
10. An opportunity owner changes its evidence requirement after players have applied.

For each case, reviewers must state the exact proposition, acceptable source, decision authority, consequence of uncertainty, appeal path and safeguarding boundary.

## 9. Open decisions before ratification

- Which opportunity categories exist in the MVP, and which are expressly out of scope?
- Which requirements are genuinely mandatory versus preferences?
- Who has authority to publish or change each requirement?
- What evidence can informal coaches reasonably provide without excluding grassroots structures?
- What organization and league sources are authoritative for each formal registration claim?
- What level of identity checking is proportionate for coaches, representatives and reviewers?
- When must corroboration be independent, and how are common-source dependencies detected?
- What is the dispute, correction, appeal and supersession process?
- What data can be disclosed to whom, for what purpose and for how long?
- How do child voice, guardian authority, consent and safeguarding obligations interact under qualified South African legal review?

No numeric trust score, universal evidence threshold, or blanket formal-registration prerequisite is proposed.

## 10. Gate and implementation boundary

**No implementation is authorized.** This matrix does not change:
- the review engine's existing verified-membership requirement;
- fixtures, schemas, JSON contracts, database boundaries, RLS, API or UI behavior;
- membership or opportunity lifecycle states;
- guardian/consent/safeguarding rules;
- mainline gates or implementation holds.

Before ratification, this matrix must be challenged by real grassroots participants and the relevant organization/competition actors. Any legal, privacy, child-safeguarding or competition-rule question must be reviewed by a suitably qualified authority. Only an explicit governance decision may authorize reconciliation into the Domain Model and downstream artifacts.

**Current decision:** retain the existing rule and treat all rows in this matrix as hypotheses for validation.

---

**Evidence principle:** Evidence is accepted for a proposition, by an authorized decision-maker, within a defined period and scope. It is never a general certificate of a player's worth.
