Review principles

These are evidence dimensions, not automated trust decisions.

1 · ProvenanceWho supplied it, where it originated, and how it was obtained.
2 · AuthorityWhether the source is competent and authorized for this specific claim.
3 · CurrencyWhen it was issued and whether it remains valid now.
4 · IndependenceWhether corroboration is genuinely independent.
5 · ContradictionsConflicting claims remain visible and unresolved until adjudicated.
Hard boundary: this prototype stores only fictional evidence descriptions. It does not upload, open, validate, authenticate or retain real documents.
No opportunity can become publishable from this screen. Human authority, safeguarding, legal and access-policy gates remain separate.
