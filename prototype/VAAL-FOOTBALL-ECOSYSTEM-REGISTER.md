# Vaal Football Ecosystem Register

**Status:** research seed register — NOT a complete or verified census  
**First operating partner target:** SAFA Emfuleni / Emfuleni Local Football Association (ELFA)  
**Geographic ambition:** Vaal / Sedibeng, then Gauteng, then South Africa  
**As-of date:** 16 November 2026  
**Branch:** `prototype/internal-demo`

## 1. Purpose

ScoutSA must account for the football ecosystem, not only players who already appear in a formal league. This register is the working inventory for discovering and reconciling:

- senior and junior clubs, academies and development teams;
- women's and girls' teams;
- schools and school teams;
- leagues, streams, tournaments and festivals;
- coaches, referees, scouts and technical staff;
- venues and training grounds;
- football photographers, videographers, editors and other creators;
- legitimate academies, agencies and opportunity providers.

**Important:** a team appearing in a news report or tournament does not prove it is currently active, affiliated, in good standing, or available for ScoutSA onboarding. Every row below is a research lead until directly confirmed with the team and, where relevant, the competent football structure.

## 2. Geographic coverage model

Do not collapse all Vaal football into one undifferentiated list.

### Initial ELFA-first coverage zones

- Evaton
- Sebokeng (including its zones and surrounding settlements)
- Vereeniging
- Vanderbijlpark
- Sharpeville
- Boipatong
- Bophelong
- Boitshepiville
- Other Emfuleni localities identified by ELFA and community mapping

The 2024 report describes four ELFA development streams: Evaton, Sebokeng, Vereeniging and Vanderbijlpark. A July 2025 report for the 2025/26 Development League describes four streams: Evaton, Sebokeng, Boitshepiville and Vereeniging. A November 2026 report describes two Super League streams: Purple (Sharpeville, Boipatong, Bophelong and Vanderbijlpark) and Green (Sebokeng and Evaton). Treat these as dated, competition-specific structures; do not assume the names or boundaries are interchangeable. Obtain the current season's authoritative stream map from ELFA.

### Expansion boundary

The wider Vaal/Sedibeng discovery map should separately identify:
- Emfuleni / ELFA;
- Midvaal / MLFA;
- Lesedi / LLFA;
- independent or non-affiliated community football;
- school football and university / college football;
- clubs and tournaments that travel into the Vaal from outside the district.

Sasolburg and other cross-provincial Vaal references must be tagged by municipality and province rather than silently grouped into Emfuleni.

## 3. Evidence labels

| Label | Meaning | What it does NOT mean |
|---|---|---|
| Reported current | Mentioned in a recent report about a current season or event | Official membership or current good standing |
| Reported event participant | Named as taking part in a dated tournament | Year-round activity |
| Historical lead | Mentioned in an older fixture, league or event report | Still operating |
| Directly confirmed | Confirmed with an authorised team/association contact and recorded with date | Legal or safeguarding clearance |
| Affiliation verified | Affiliation independently checked with the relevant association | Player identity or safeguarding verified |
| Dormant / merged / renamed | Status confirmed by an authorised source | Delete history; retain aliases and lineage |
| Disputed / unresolved | Conflicting identity, status or affiliation evidence | Choose whichever source looks newest without review |

## 8. Required coverage ledger

- Association list requested
- List reconciled

A locality cannot be marked complete merely because ScoutSA has found several clubs there. Completion requires comparison against the current association register, league/competition lists, tournament organisers, school sport structures and community discovery.

## 9. Reconciliation protocol

For each candidate:
1. Preserve the name exactly as it appears in the source.
2. Record aliases separately; never merge on name similarity alone.
3. Record locality and venue separately.
4. Record the competition and season, not merely “football club”.
5. Ask the relevant association whether the team is affiliated and active in the current season.
6. Ask the team to confirm its authorised representative and public contact channel.
7. Record the date, source, reviewer and unresolved contradictions.
8. Re-check each season; teams can fold, merge, rename, change status or move competitions.

## 10. Coverage metrics

Report these separately:
- candidate teams found;
- teams with direct confirmation;
- teams with current-season participation evidence;
- affiliations independently checked;
- teams with a verified coach contact;
- teams by locality, age group and gender;
- schools and school teams mapped;
- tournaments mapped and organisers contacted;
- unresolved identity / alias conflicts;
- localities with no reliable coverage evidence.

Never publish “100% of Vaal teams covered” unless the denominator and source universe are defined and independently reconciled. A more honest public statement is “X teams identified and Y confirmed across Z mapped competitions/localities as of [date].”
