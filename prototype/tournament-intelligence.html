Stats need a defined metric, unit, match context, recorder/source, timestamp and review state. A statistic is evidence to inspect—not a talent verdict or a guarantee of opportunity.

Primary IDs identify records; foreign keys connect them. Many-to-many relationships use junction records such as tournament team entries and player participations. Never use a person's name as a database key.

Organizer directory records should separately track organization identity, organizer authority, public contact source, permission to feature, listing status and last-confirmed date. A public event report is a research lead—not permission to publish or proof that an event is current.
