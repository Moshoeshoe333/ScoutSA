# Opportunity Verification Procedure v1.0 — Claim-Scoped States and Blocking

**Effective decision date:** 2026-10-09  
**Status:** RATIFIED FOR SYNTHETIC MANUAL TABLETOP PROCEDURE ONLY  
**Ratification record:** `docs/research/opportunity-verification-rule-ratification-2026-10-09.md`  
**Scope:** Manual synthetic tabletop evaluation; no real-world operational authority  
**Authority boundary:** This procedure does not amend the Constitution, authorize implementation, establish software enforcement, or authorize a rehearsal, publication, outreach, or live pilot.

## 1. Governing distinction

**Authenticity is not actionability.** Corroborating that an organizer or invitation is genuine does not prove that a place is available, a participant is eligible, the costs are known, the participant can attend, or the specific action is consent-authorized.

The generic word **verified** must not be used to imply a complete opportunity or participant-specific readiness unless the evidence supports the exact scope. State transitions are deliberate, recorded decisions—not automatic progression.

## 2. Three verification tiers

### Tier 1 — AUTHENTICITY CORROBORATED (LIMITED SCOPE)

Only specifically enumerated claims may be marked corroborated. The record must include all of the following:
- claim-by-claim scope: exactly what was and was not checked;
- source/channel and a source-packet or evidence reference;
- timestamp of the verification action;
- verifier identity or accountable reviewer identifier;
- limitations, conflicts, and unresolved claims.

A missing required field means this tier is **not established**. Do not fill missing provenance by inference. A dated evaluation record is not a substitute for a missing timestamp of the underlying verification action.

This tier authorizes internal retention and further verification work only. It does not authorize public visibility, participant-facing claims, invitations, or a claim that the opportunity is fully verified.

### Tier 2 — OPPORTUNITY CONDITIONS CHECKED (SCOPE LIMITED)

Before this tier can be recorded, evidence and provenance must support the material conditions relevant to the stated use. Record a source reference, verification timestamp, verifier identity, scope, and limitations for each material condition.

At minimum, assess and record as applicable:
- event date, time, venue, and responsible receiving organization;
- eligibility criteria;
- actual capacity / whether a place is available;
- fee policy, amount, recipient, and payment conditions, including explicit confirmation when no fee is required where that is material;
- **transport/access arrangements, responsibility, and known costs for any opportunity requiring physical attendance**;
- safeguarding and guardian conditions relevant to the activity;
- the authorized contact route, known dependencies, unresolved facts, and review limitations.

Eligibility, capacity, and transport/access/cost are blocking conditions for a physical opportunity when relevant. A condition cannot be treated as satisfied merely because no contrary evidence was supplied. If any material condition remains unresolved, the opportunity stays **PAUSED / BLOCKED — NON-ACTIONABLE** and must not be published or used to initiate player-specific contact.

This tier is not a guarantee of selection, overall safety, universal suitability, or participant-specific feasibility.

### Tier 3 — ACTIONABLE FOR A PARTICULAR PARTICIPANT

This tier requires Tier 1 and Tier 2 evidence to be sufficient for the specific proposed action, plus participant-specific eligibility, feasibility, applicable consent/guardian authority, purpose and disclosure scope, and any other applicable requirements.

Record a source reference, verification timestamp, verifier identity, participant-specific scope, limitations, and the explicit decision authorizing the proposed action. Missing any required evidence field or applicable condition blocks this tier.

An opportunity cannot be marked **Verified** for participant-facing or actionable use unless authenticity is corroborated **and** the opportunity is actionable for that particular participant. No tier automatically grants permission to progress to the next.

## 3. Downgrade, pause, and conflict rule

If required provenance is missing, evidence conflicts, or new information weakens an earlier conclusion, do not preserve a higher tier by default. Downgrade to the last evidence-supported scope, or mark **PAUSED / BLOCKED** where the missing or conflicting fact prevents a safe decision. Preserve prior states and explain the transition; do not silently overwrite history.

Do not infer football ability, commitment, or talent from transport, cost, scheduling, or other access barriers.

Uncertainty alone is not a finding of misconduct. Rejection/closure requires a documented basis or a separately approved closure rule.

## 4. Clarification and escalation

For a material unresolved condition, record the targeted clarification requested, date/time of request, source/contact route, responsible reviewer, response or non-response, and current block.

If the condition is not resolved within **72 hours**, escalate to the **designated authority** and record the escalation timestamp, route, recipient role, and response. If no designated authority has been formally assigned, record that governance gap and do not invent a person's identity or authority. If unresolved after escalation, the case remains **BLOCKED**; it does not become verified by elapsed time.

The 72-hour period is an escalation trigger, not permission to publish, contact a participant, assume consent, waive a requirement, or close a case automatically. No live operational escalation is authorized by this synthetic tabletop procedure.

The fixture describes a submitted invitation, a separate confirmation through a predesignated official channel represented in the synthetic packet, and unresolved eligibility, final capacity, and transport arrangements. The only potential Tier 1 scope is the organizer identity and precisely corroborated invitation details. The fixture reference is recorded, but the initial observation does not provide the underlying verification-action timestamp or a sufficiently explicit verifier identifier. Therefore Tier 1 is **not established by the current record**.

Do not infer missing evidence, amend the frozen oracle, publish the opportunity, surface player profiles, or issue a participant-specific invitation. Retest only when the required evidence is actually available and recorded.

The frozen fixture's 12-month threshold is not established by the inspected governing documents. This procedure does not ratify that threshold. STS-006 is **BLOCKED FROM EVALUATION** pending a separately governed freshness rule or an approved fixture-version change. Do not use the fixture itself as its own governing authority, and do not silently alter frozen v1.0.

## 7. Procedure and evaluation integrity

- Keep fixture inputs and expected outcomes frozen.
- Record the observation before comparing it with the expected outcome.
- Use PASS, FAIL, NOT TESTED, or BLOCKED with evidence and rationale; do not turn missing evidence into PASS.
- Preserve the original observation, any blocked result, corrective action, and retest separately.
- A manual tabletop evaluates the written/manual procedure only; it is not software-enforcement, legal-compliance, or live-pilot evidence.
