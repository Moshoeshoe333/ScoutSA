# Gate 2 G2-SEAM-06 — Child Participation / Voice Ratification Record

**Date:** 2026-10-06  
**Seam:** G2-SEAM-06  
**Decision:** RATIFIED — semantic domain concept

## 1. Ratification basis

The G2-SEAM-06 re-witness and ratification review established that:

- Gate 1 M8 already requires child voice to remain distinct from Guardian authority and final decision.
- ConsentRecord cannot safely represent child voice because consent and expressed participation are not equivalent.
- AuditEvent cannot safely represent the substantive participation relationship.
- A first-class Child Participation / Voice Record closes the witnessed semantic gap without inventing legal capacity, consent, Guardian precedence, child veto, or AI authority.
- All ratification tests passed.

## 2. Ratified concept

**Child Participation / Voice Record** is an attributable record of a child's expressed view or participation in a defined matter/context, preserved with provenance and temporal context and kept distinct from Guardian authority, ConsentRecord, and any resulting Decision.

Where applicable, it may preserve the matter/purpose/context, expressing actor, time, provenance, relationship to Guardian authority/ConsentRecord, resulting Decision reference, correction/dispute history, and access classification.

These are semantic requirements, not database columns.

## 3. Ratified boundaries

- CHILD VOICE ≠ GUARDIAN AUTHORITY
- CHILD VOICE ≠ CONSENT
- CHILD VOICE ≠ FINAL DECISION
- CHILD OBJECTION ≠ AUTOMATIC VETO
- GUARDIAN AUTHORITY ≠ AUTOMATIC SUPPRESSION OF CHILD VOICE
- HISTORICAL CHILD EXPRESSION ≠ LATER DECISION
- AI ≠ CAPACITY ASSESSOR
- AI ≠ BEST-INTERESTS ADJUDICATOR

## 4. Explicit non-ratifications

This ratification does not establish:

- fixed age thresholds;
- legal capacity;
- universal assent rules;
- universal consent rules;
- universal child veto;
- universal Guardian precedence;
- automatic conflict resolution;
- AI determination of capacity or best interests;
- POPIA/Children's Act compliance certification;
- database schema;
- RLS;
- API;
- UI;
- production readiness.

The contextual effect of a child's view remains subject to applicable law, policy, safeguarding context, and competent process.

## 5. Reconciliation authorization

The ratification authorizes semantic reconciliation:

**Constitution → Domain Model → Relationship Model → Ledger**

No ERD/schema/implementation work is authorized merely by this ratification.
