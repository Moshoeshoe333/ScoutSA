# ScoutSA External Validation Plan — 2026-10-06

**AuthorityLevel:** NONE  
**Status:** VALIDATION PLAN — NOT EVIDENCE  
**Purpose:** Replace further internal governance expansion with bounded external evidence gathering.

## 1. Objective

Test whether ScoutSA's current product thesis and MVP boundaries correspond to real needs among Vaal Triangle football stakeholders.

This plan seeks disconfirming evidence, not endorsement.

## 2. Minimum five conversations

Target exactly five initial conversations before creating another internal gate artifact:

1. Two grassroots club coaches.
2. One school sports coordinator.
3. One guardian of a youth player.
4. One scout / academy recruiter.

No participant is treated as representative of South African football generally.

## 3. Core question

Ask:

> When you hear about a talented young player, what actually stops them from getting to a trial or opportunity?

Do not lead with transport, guardianship, academics, cost or Opportunity Readiness. Let the participant identify the barriers first.

## 4. Secondary questions

- What information do you need before contacting a player?
- What information would you refuse to receive or store?
- How do you currently verify player information?
- What makes a player/opportunity trustworthy?
- What makes a trial request workable or unworkable?
- What role do guardians play in practice?
- What safeguarding concerns arise in current workflows?
- What causes opportunities to fail after a player is identified?
- What would make ScoutSA useful enough to use repeatedly?
- What would make you reject it?

## 5. Evidence discipline

Each conversation should preserve participant category, date, context, questions, attributable notes or transcript where permission allows, direct observations, interpretation, uncertainty, and contradictions with current assumptions.

External feedback is evidence input, not automatic ratification.

## 6. Falsification tests

**F-01 Opportunity Readiness:** If stakeholders identify barriers unrelated to transport, guardian availability, academic constraints or practical access, reconsider the Opportunity Readiness thesis.

**F-02 Player Passport:** If stakeholders cannot identify a useful decision they would make from a Passport, revise the Passport model.

**F-03 Verification:** If stakeholders do not trust ScoutSA-style verification without institutional relationships, pursue external-partner design rather than more internal semantics.

**F-04 Opportunity workflow:** If coaches/scouts prefer existing channels and see no meaningful discovery/contact/trial friction, reconsider the opportunity-network thesis.

**F-05 Safeguarding workflow:** If proposed Guardian/contact controls are impractical in real workflows, change the workflow rather than expecting users to adapt to documentation.

## 7. External legal review

Seek one narrowly scoped South African legal review covering the Consent Model, MVP access-policy matrix, and relevant POPIA / Children's Act assumptions.

Questions for counsel:

1. Which assumptions are legally unsafe or incomplete?
2. Which processing/access distinctions require a lawful basis beyond consent?
3. What Guardian/child authority assumptions require correction?
4. What minimum disclosure/access controls should be treated as legal requirements?
5. What retention/deletion issues must be resolved before implementation?
6. Which safeguarding records require special handling?

The review is an external input, not a compliance certification.

## 8. Anti-ceremony rule

Do not create a new witness/re-witness/ratification artifact for each conversation.

After the five conversations and legal review, create one synthesis artifact only if the evidence produces decisions or contradictions requiring preservation.

If three internal artifacts would otherwise be needed to resolve one uncertainty, escalate to an external human instead.

## 9. Success condition

The validation phase succeeds if it produces a confirmed problem with stronger evidence, a materially revised thesis, a materially reduced MVP, a new operational/legal constraint, evidence that a proposed feature is unnecessary, or evidence that a real stakeholder would use the workflow.

“No change” is also a valid finding if supported by evidence.

## 10. Exit condition

Do not proceed to production RLS or production database implementation merely because conversations are positive.

External validation must first be reconciled with the MVP boundary, access-policy matrix, legal review and implementation prerequisites.

**Decision:** EXTERNAL VALIDATION PHASE — OPEN.

**Internal governance expansion:** FROZEN UNLESS NEW EVIDENCE REQUIRES IT.
