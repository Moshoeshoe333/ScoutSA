# ScoutSA Gate 1 — Candidate Amendment Re-Witness

**Status:** Preliminary adversarial re-witness — NOT RATIFIED  
**Date:** 2026-10-05  
**Basis:** Gate 1 Cases 01–14 + external/legal anchor research + candidate amendment draft

## 1. Purpose

Test whether the six candidate amendment themes survive adversarial review without:

- inventing legal precedence;
- creating an absolute retention obligation;
- collapsing distinct purposes;
- converting derived state into authority;
- or introducing implementation requirements prematurely.

## 2. M1 — Epistemic Object Separation

**Affected cases:** 1, 10, 13.

### Existence
PASS AS SEMANTIC PRINCIPLE. The witnesses repeatedly distinguish actor assertions, evidence, verification and derived state.

### Coherence
PASS. No conflict with the existing Constitution. The distinction strengthens the evidence rule.

### Failure
RED if an assertion is silently treated as proof merely because its author is authenticated, institutional or verified.

### Re-witness result
**RETAIN — candidate survives.**

### Boundary
This is a product epistemic rule, not a claim that every assertion requires a particular database object.

## 3. M2 — Scoped Authority and Conflict Preservation

**Affected cases:** 2, 8, 13.

### Existence
PASS AS PRINCIPLE. GuardianLink and authority scope exist; the missing part is complete conflict semantics.

### Coherence
PASS WITH LEGAL-REVIEW BOUNDARY. Children's Act section 18 demonstrates that parental responsibilities and rights may be specific and that multiple guardians can exist, subject to statutory/court conditions. This supports scoped authority but does not provide a universal ScoutSA precedence algorithm.

### Failure
RED if ScoutSA selects a winner from role labels, recency, verification level or AI inference alone.

### Re-witness result
**RETAIN — but explicitly require applicable law/court order/policy where precedence is legally determined.**

## 4. M3 — Purpose-Bound Authorization

**Affected cases:** 3, 6, 7, 8, 11.

### Existence
PASS AS PRINCIPLE. Consent is already purpose-bound and revocable.

### Coherence
PASS. POPIA purpose specification and further-processing provisions support purpose-sensitive processing. Child processing receives additional protection.

### Failure
RED if trial participation is silently converted into media, publication, contact or secondary-use permission.

### Re-witness result
**RETAIN — candidate survives.**

### Boundary
The final purpose taxonomy remains product policy plus legal review; no enum is implied.

## 5. M4 — Temporal Applicability and Freshness

**Affected cases:** 3, 4, 5, 12.

### Existence
PASS. Evidence, verification, consent and readiness already have temporal concepts.

### Coherence
PASS AS PRINCIPLE. POPIA information-quality requirements support keeping information accurate and updated where necessary, with regard to purpose.

### Failure
RED if authentic historical evidence is treated as automatically current or sufficient.

### Re-witness result
**RETAIN — with a necessary qualification:** historical reconstruction is subject to applicable retention/deletion law and policy. The clause must not become an absolute claim that every historical personal-information record must always be retained.

## 6. M5 — Derived-State Dependency and Invalidation

**Affected cases:** 4, 5, 12, 14.

### Existence
PASS AS PRINCIPLE. Opportunity Readiness is already defined as derived and point-in-time.

### Coherence
PASS. A current derived state cannot logically be stronger than the material inputs on which it depends.

### Failure
RED if revoked consent, expired evidence, changed opportunity requirements or another material withdrawal leaves an old readiness result functioning as current authorization.

### Re-witness result
**RETAIN — candidate survives, but implementation mechanics remain open.**

## 7. M6 — Safeguarding Distinct From Verification

**Affected cases:** 6, 9.

### Existence
PASS AS EXTERNAL DIRECTION. Constitution section 28, Children's Act principles, FIFA safeguarding policy/guidance and SAFA's retrieved academy regulation all support safeguarding as a distinct concern.

### Coherence
PASS. Verification of identity or organizational status does not logically establish that a protected interaction is safe or authorised.

### Failure
RED if a verified Scout/organization user can bypass safeguarding controls merely because identity or role verification succeeded.

### Re-witness result
**RETAIN — candidate survives.**

### Boundary
ScoutSA must not invent a universal legal definition of safeguarding clearance.

## 8. M7 — Disclosure / Republication Boundary

**Affected cases:** 7, 11, 12.

### Existence
PASS AS PRINCIPLE. Public/discoverable information is already distinguished from controlled Passport data.

### Coherence
PASS WITH LEGAL REVIEW. POPIA's further-processing framework includes circumstances involving public records, but that does not establish unrestricted product permission for every new use or child-data context.

### Failure
RED if public availability alone is treated as blanket permission to aggregate, republish or reuse information.

### Re-witness result
**RETAIN — candidate survives with legal-review qualification.**

## 9. Cross-cutting overreach test

### O1 — Absolute retention
**REJECTED.** Historical provenance must remain reconstructable where lawful and appropriate, but no candidate clause creates an unconditional retention mandate.

### O2 — Universal Guardian winner
**REJECTED.** No candidate clause creates one.

### O3 — Universal consent requirement
**REJECTED.** The candidates require applicable purpose, authority and law to be evaluated; they do not say every action requires the same consent.

### O4 — Automatic invalidation implementation
**REJECTED.** The semantic requirement is current-state correctness; triggers, jobs, event sourcing or recalculation mechanisms remain unselected.

### O5 — AI authority
**REJECTED.** No candidate clause permits AI to create legal or safeguarding authority.

### O6 — Compliance claim
**REJECTED.** External support is evidence for domain constraints, not proof that ScoutSA is compliant.

## 10. Re-witness verdict

All six candidate amendment themes survive this preliminary adversarial pass **as semantic candidates**, with three explicit qualifications:

1. historical preservation is subject to applicable retention/deletion law and policy;
2. authority conflict requires applicable legal/policy resolution rather than invented precedence;
3. public-information/republication behavior remains purpose- and context-dependent.

Therefore:

**Candidate amendments: RETAIN FOR FORMAL DRAFTING**

**Constitution amendment: NOT YET**

**Domain Model amendment: NOT YET**

**ERD/schema: DEFERRED**

**Implementation: DEFERRED**

**Gate 1: OPEN**
