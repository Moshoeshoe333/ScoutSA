# Proposal — Opportunity Verification States and Blocking Conditions

**Date:** 2026-10-09  
**Status:** PROPOSAL ONLY — NOT RATIFIED  
**Scope:** Manual synthetic tabletop procedure clarification  
**Authority effect:** None until explicitly ratified through the project's governance process. No implementation or live use is authorized.

## 1. Problem statement

STS-001 shows that independent corroboration of an organizer or specific invitation does not resolve every condition needed to represent an opportunity as complete, feasible, or actionable. The current documents state the principles but do not consistently name the operational states or define which missing fields block progression.

This proposal does not change the frozen STS-001 oracle. It proposes a procedure clarification to be reviewed and, if accepted, versioned before the affected scenario is retested.

## 2. Proposed claim-scoped states

1. **UNVERIFIED** — no adequate independent corroboration exists for the particular claim.
2. **AUTHENTICITY CORROBORATED — LIMITED SCOPE** — the organizer identity and/or specified invitation details have been corroborated through an independently obtained official channel. The record must enumerate exactly which claims were checked, the source/channel, source packet or evidence reference, timestamp, reviewer, and limitations. This state is not equivalent to full opportunity verification.
3. **PAUSED — MATERIAL CONDITIONS UNRESOLVED** — one or more material eligibility, capacity, date/location, cost, transport, safeguarding, or responsibility conditions are unresolved. The opportunity must not be described as fully verified or actionable. It must not be made public or used to initiate player-specific contact while the material condition blocks progression.
4. **OPPORTUNITY CONDITIONS CHECKED — SCOPE LIMITED** — the specified conditions have supporting evidence, but this label must not imply guaranteed selection, overall safety, or universal suitability.
5. **REJECTED / CLOSED** — a documented basis supports rejection or the case is closed under a separately approved closure rule. Uncertainty alone must not be rewritten as a finding of misconduct.

These are proposed manual labels, not existing software states.

## 3. Proposed blocking rule

Independent corroboration is claim-specific. It permits only the corroborated claim(s) to be marked checked. It does not automatically verify the event's eligibility rules, capacity, access arrangements, fees, or suitability.

Before an opportunity can be described as actionable for a particular participant or made available for player-facing use, every condition material to that use must be supported by evidence and recorded. At minimum, where relevant, record:
- date, time, and venue;
- participant eligibility criteria;
- capacity or confirmation of available place;
- fees and who receives payment, if any;
- transport/access responsibility and known costs;
- receiving-organization contact/authority and verification provenance;
- safeguarding or guardian conditions that apply to the proposed activity;
- limitations, reviewer, and timestamp.

If a material condition remains unresolved, keep the opportunity paused and non-actionable. Do not infer that a transport barrier is evidence of football ability or commitment. If no response or an ambiguous response is received, retain the unresolved state; do not invent an expiry period until a time-bound escalation/closure rule is separately approved.

## 4. Proposed handling of STS-001

The evaluator may record the organizer identity and precisely corroborated invitation details as checked only if the source scope and timestamp are evidenced. Eligibility, capacity, and transport remain unresolved and must be recorded as such. The opportunity remains paused and non-actionable. No player-specific invitation or public listing is permitted on the basis of this packet.

This approach preserves claim-level evidence while preventing limited corroboration from being overstated as complete verification.

## 5. Open decisions requiring ratification

- Approve, amend, or reject the proposed labels and blocking rule.
- Decide whether any additional conditions are always mandatory versus context-dependent.
- Decide the accountable operational role for clarification and escalation.
- Define a time-bound review/closure rule, or explicitly defer it without inventing a deadline.
- Confirm how this manual procedure will be versioned and retested against STS-001.

It does not establish legal compliance, software enforcement, or pilot readiness. No policy amendment is in force unless separately ratified and recorded.
