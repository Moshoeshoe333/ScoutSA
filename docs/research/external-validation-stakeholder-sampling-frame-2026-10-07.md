**Status:** RESEARCH / FIELD-ACCESS PREPARATION — NOT EVIDENCE OF PRODUCT VALIDATION  
**AuthorityLevel:** NONE  
**Purpose:** Identify a small, diverse first-contact sample for the external-validation interview pack without treating public information as stakeholder endorsement.

## 1. Sampling rule

The first five conversations should deliberately cover different positions in the grassroots opportunity chain:

1. Grassroots coach / club operator
2. Academy / development coach
3. School sports perspective
4. Guardian perspective
5. Scout / academy recruitment perspective

A single organization may cover more than one perspective, but the participant's actual role must be recorded.

The sample is **not statistically representative** of the Vaal Triangle. It is a purposive discovery sample intended to expose contradictions in the current ScoutSA assumptions.

## 3. Additional scout/recruitment route

If the first five conversations do not produce a genuine scout/recruitment perspective, recruit one separately from an academy or development environment with a documented player pathway.

Do not equate:
- coach = scout;
- academy = scout;
- player success = proof of ScoutSA's thesis.

The participant's actual role and workflow must be recorded.

## 4. Contact discipline

Initial contact should:

- introduce the work as research, not a sales pitch;
- request a short adult stakeholder conversation;
- avoid asking for names, contact details or case histories of minors;
- avoid requesting confidential club, school or safeguarding information;
- avoid promising partnership, funding, exposure, trials or placements;
- avoid describing ScoutSA as legally compliant;
- use the neutral interview pack before concept reaction;
- obtain permission before recording or retaining notes.

## 5. Evidence boundary

Public-source evidence establishes only that an organization or channel appears relevant enough to approach.

It does **not** establish:

- agreement with ScoutSA;
- a stated problem;
- demand for ScoutSA;
- willingness to adopt;
- product-market fit;
- safeguarding quality;
- legal compliance;
- commercial viability;
- representativeness of the Vaal Triangle.

Those claims require direct evidence.

## 7. Evidence record to create after each conversation

Use the existing interview pack. Do not create a new governance gate.

Record:

- participant category;
- date/context;
- permission to take notes/record;
- direct observations;
- current workflow;
- barriers named spontaneously;
- trust/verification process;
- information participants need;
- information they would refuse to receive/store;
- guardian/safeguarding workflow;
- existing tools;
- concept reaction;
- contradictions;
- confidence;
- action classification.
