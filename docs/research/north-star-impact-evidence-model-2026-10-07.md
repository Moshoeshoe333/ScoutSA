# ScoutSA North-Star Impact Evidence Model — 2026-10-07

## Status

**RESEARCH / DESIGN INPUT — NOT RATIFIED**

This artifact defines a conceptual evidence model for evaluating whether ScoutSA creates meaningful football opportunity outcomes. It does not authorize implementation, database changes, RLS, migration, production deployment, investor claims, or legal/compliance claims.

**AuthorityLevel: NONE beyond the evidence recorded here.**

## North Star

**Verified players receiving genuine football opportunities.**

Platform activity such as registrations, profile views, searches, messages, or downloads is not by itself evidence of meaningful opportunity.

## Proposed Evidence Ladder

**Player existence → Evidence-supported information → Relevant opportunity exposure → Authorized connection → Genuine opportunity event → Real-world outcome**

Each level answers a different question and must not silently imply the next level.

### 1. Player existence

Evidence that a Player Passport exists. This establishes system entry only.

### 2. Evidence-supported information

A material player claim has attributable evidence and/or scoped verification. This does not establish suitability for every opportunity.

### 3. Relevant opportunity exposure

A player was shown or considered for an opportunity within applicable visibility boundaries. This does not establish that contact occurred or that the opportunity was genuine.

### 4. Authorized connection

A contact or trial pathway was initiated through the applicable authorization process. This does not establish attendance, selection, or placement.

### 5. Genuine opportunity event

There is evidence that a real-world opportunity occurred, such as an invited or attended trial, with sufficient context to distinguish it from a platform event.

This does not establish successful selection or placement.

### 6. Real-world outcome

There is sufficient evidence of a meaningful downstream outcome, such as a completed football pathway or placement where legitimately recordable. The exact definition remains open for external validation.

## Attribution Discipline

For every claimed outcome, ask:

1. Did the opportunity exist?
2. What happened before ScoutSA involvement?
3. What happened through ScoutSA?
4. What happened outside ScoutSA?
5. What evidence connects ScoutSA activity to the outcome?
6. Are there competing explanations?
7. Who reported or verified the outcome?
8. What remains unresolved?

**Correlation is not causation.** A sequence of records in ScoutSA does not automatically prove that ScoutSA caused the outcome.

## Failure Evidence

The model should preserve meaningful failures and constraints, including declined requests, unresolved authorization, inability to attend, transport or scheduling constraints, insufficient verification, cancelled opportunities, safeguarding-related actions, and unknown outcomes.

A constraint encountered is not automatically a product defect. A safe refusal is not automatically a failed product outcome.

## Diagnostic Funnel

**Registered → Evidence-supported → Relevant exposure → Authorized connection → Genuine opportunity → Outcome**

Each stage requires its own denominator and evidence standard. No target values are proposed at this stage.

Potential future diagnostic ratios include evidence-supported / registered, authorized connections / relevant exposures, genuine opportunity events / authorized connections, and outcomes / genuine opportunity events. These are research candidates only.

## External Validation Questions

- What counts as a genuine football opportunity in practice?
- What evidence would convince a stakeholder that it actually happened?
- What outcome matters after a trial?
- Which outcomes are normally recorded today?
- Who can reliably confirm them?
- Where do opportunities disappear from the current process?
- Which parts of this pathway are already handled effectively through clubs, schools, academies, WhatsApp, or other informal networks?

## Minimum Evidence Rule

**Claim strength must never exceed evidence strength.**

The stronger the claim, especially a claim about opportunity creation or placement attributable to ScoutSA, the stronger and more independent the supporting evidence must be.

## Conclusion

ScoutSA should ultimately be able to describe a defensible pathway:

**Who entered → what was evidenced → what opportunity became relevant → what authorization occurred → what real-world event happened → what outcome followed → what evidence supports the account.**

The strongest impact claim is the smallest claim the evidence can honestly support.

## Ratification Boundary

External stakeholder evidence must test this model before any metric becomes a ratified product requirement. No production analytics, schema change, RLS change, migration, or production claim follows from this document alone.
