## 1. Purpose

This re-witness tests whether the formal seven-amendment proposal actually addresses the witnessed cases without:

- silently resolving conflicts;
- creating unsupported legal precedence;
- erasing history;
- promoting derived state to authority;
- converting public availability into permission;
- converting verification into safeguarding clearance;
- or leaking into implementation decisions.

A surviving amendment is not a ratified rule.

## 3. 33×3 Findings

### Case 01 — Player vs Coach Assertion

**Existence:** The proposal explicitly distinguishes assertion, evidence, verification, derived state and decision.

**Coherence:** This strengthens the existing evidence rule rather than changing it.

**Failure:** An authenticated or verified actor still cannot turn an assertion into truth merely by status.

**Verdict:** **RETAIN.**

---

### Case 02 — Guardian Authority Conflict

**Existence:** Scoped authority, temporal state and purpose-bound authorization already exist conceptually and are strengthened.

**Coherence:** The proposal refuses to manufacture a universal Guardian precedence rule.

**Failure:** The dangerous behavior remains explicitly prohibited: role label, recency, verification level or institutional status cannot silently select a winner.

**Residual:** Exact legal precedence remains unresolved.

**Verdict:** **RETAIN — LEGAL/POLICY RESOLUTION REQUIRED.**

---

### Case 03 — Consent Revoked After Approval

**Existence:** Purpose-bound, revocable and temporal consent is already modeled.

**Coherence:** The proposal distinguishes authorization at the historical evaluation/disclosure time from current authorization.

**Failure:** Later revocation cannot erase the historical fact of prior authorization, nor can prior authorization automatically authorize a new action.

**Verdict:** **RETAIN.**

---

### Case 04 — Evidence Expires After Verification

**Existence:** Evidence and verification already have temporal concepts.

**Coherence:** Authentic historical support is distinct from current sufficiency.

**Failure:** A prior verification cannot silently remain sufficient for a current decision after its material supporting evidence expires.

**Residual:** Exact freshness periods remain intentionally unresolved.

**Verdict:** **RETAIN — FRESHNESS POLICY REQUIRED LATER.**

---

### Case 05 — Opportunity Changes After Readiness

**Existence:** Opportunity Readiness is contextual and point-in-time.

**Coherence:** A readiness result remains a historical result tied to its evaluation context; it does not automatically describe the changed Opportunity.

**Failure:** A material Opportunity change cannot silently leave an old readiness result functioning as current.

**Residual:** Exact materiality criteria and reconstruction mechanics remain unresolved.

**Verdict:** **RETAIN — DOMAIN DETAIL REQUIRED LATER.**

---

### Case 06 — Unverified Scout Contacts Minor

**Existence:** Role, verification, discovery, consent and guardian/safeguarding concepts are distinguishable.

**Coherence:** The proposal separates role/verification from protected contact authority and safeguarding.

**Failure:** A Scout role or discoverability cannot silently become authorization to contact a minor or access protected information.

**Verdict:** **RETAIN.**

---

### Case 07 — Guardian Authorizes Trial but Not Media

**Existence:** Consent is purpose-specific.

**Coherence:** Trial participation, media, publication and secondary use remain distinct authorization purposes.

**Failure:** Trial authorization cannot silently become media/publication permission.

**Verdict:** **RETAIN.**

---

### Case 08 — Child View vs Guardian Authorization

**Existence:** Guardian authority, consent and minor safeguards are present.

**Coherence:** The proposal correctly refuses to treat either role labels or consent possession as universal authority.

**Failure:** The proposal prevents silent promotion, but it does not itself answer the separate question of how ScoutSA should treat:

- a child's objection;
- a child's assent or participation preference;
- disagreement between child view and guardian authorization;
- applicable legal age/capacity rules;
- safeguarding circumstances that alter the permissible action.

**Verdict:** **RETAIN, BUT ESCALATE AS A RESIDUAL DOMAIN/LEGAL RESEARCH GAP.**

This is not a contradiction in the amendment proposal. It is an unresolved boundary that must not be invented during implementation.

---

### Case 09 — Safeguarding Concern vs Verified Organization User

**Existence:** Verification and safeguarding can be treated as separate concepts.

**Coherence:** A safeguarding concern is not automatically the same thing as a proven allegation, and organizational verification does not erase the concern.

**Failure:** Verification cannot be used as a blanket bypass of safeguarding controls.

**Residual:** Exact protective-action, investigation, escalation and resolution semantics remain unresolved.

**Verdict:** **RETAIN, BUT ESCALATE AS SAFEGUARDING CONTROL DETAIL.**

---

### Case 10 — Institutional Record vs ScoutSA Assertion

**Existence:** Evidence, source provenance, authority scope and temporal context are already recognized.

**Coherence:** Neither institutional origin nor ScoutSA ownership automatically establishes universal truth.

**Failure:** The proposal blocks silent source promotion and requires conflict/provenance preservation.

**Verdict:** **RETAIN.**

---

### Case 11 — Public Information Proposed for Republication

**Existence:** Visibility, provenance, purpose and consent concepts exist.

**Coherence:** Public availability is not treated as blanket republication authority.

**Failure:** ScoutSA cannot infer unrestricted aggregation, republication or secondary use merely from discoverability.

**Residual:** Specific legal basis, source terms, child-data treatment and operational disclosure rules remain context-dependent.

**Verdict:** **RETAIN — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED.**

---

### Case 12 — Authentic but Stale Evidence

**Existence:** Authenticity, provenance, timestamps and expiry/currentness concepts exist.

**Coherence:** Historical validity is separated from current applicability and sufficiency.

**Failure:** Authenticity alone cannot keep an old record current forever.

**Verdict:** **RETAIN.**

---

### Case 13 — Two Legitimate Authorities With Incompatible Assertions

**Existence:** Multiple actors and scoped authority are recognized.

**Coherence:** The proposal explicitly preserves conflict and refuses automatic winner selection.

**Failure:** No first-received, newest, strongest-role, organization-first, coach-first, or AI-first resolution is permitted by the proposal.

**Residual:** The actual resolution authority must come from applicable law, court order, product policy, or other legitimately authorized rule.

**Verdict:** **RETAIN — RESOLUTION RULE STILL UNRESOLVED.**

---

### Case 14 — Derived Readiness After Material Input Withdrawal

**Existence:** Readiness is already a derived, contextual, point-in-time state.

**Coherence:** The proposal makes material dependency explicit while preserving historical evaluation.

**Failure:** A stale readiness result cannot silently continue as current authorization after a material input is withdrawn.

**Residual:** Recalculation/invalidation mechanics remain implementation/domain-detail questions for a later gate.

**Verdict:** **RETAIN.**

## 5. Residual Gaps After Re-Witness

The proposal survives, but three boundaries remain explicitly unresolved:

### R-01 — Child Voice / Guardian Authorization

Case 08 demonstrates that scoped authority and purpose-bound consent are necessary but may not be sufficient to define the product treatment of a child's own view, objection, assent, or participation preference.

**Status:** RESEARCH / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED.

No implementation rule is authorized.

### R-02 — Safeguarding Protective Action

Case 09 demonstrates that verification must remain separate from safeguarding, but the exact semantics for temporary protective action, investigation, escalation, dismissal, substantiation and resolution remain unresolved.

**Status:** DOMAIN RESEARCH / SAFEGUARDING REVIEW REQUIRED.

No safeguarding-clearance enum or automated restriction rule is authorized.

### R-03 — Repository Witness Numbering Provenance

Cases 07, 08 and 09 contain duplicate numbered witness filenames representing different scenarios.

**Status:** PROVENANCE RECONCILIATION REQUIRED.

No duplicate file is deleted or silently renamed.
