## 1. Governing evidence rule

> **Strength of a claim must never exceed strength of the evidence.**

This record separates what a stakeholder directly reports from our interpretation of that report.

A contact, referral, positive reply, public-source reference, or broker/introduction event is **not** stakeholder validation by itself.

Primary evidence is strongest when a stakeholder provides a concrete, lived case that can be traced through the actual pathway from discovery toward a genuine opportunity or failure to reach one.

Do not lead the witness toward ScoutSA, transport, guardianship, cost, academics, verification, or any other proposed solution before hearing their account.

## 12. Evidence-of-value boundary

Do **not** record any of the following as product success by themselves:

- profile views;
- registrations;
- messages;
- contact requests;
- positive opinions;
- stakeholder compliments;
- social engagement;
- introductions.

Where applicable, distinguish:

**Discovery → relevant opportunity exposure → authorized connection → genuine opportunity event → real-world outcome.**

A trial invitation is not attendance. Attendance is not placement. An opportunity is not an outcome.
