# ScoutSA Gate 1 — Candidate Semantic Amendments

**Status:** Candidate research text — NOT RATIFIED  
**Date:** 2026-10-05  
**Prerequisites:** Gate 1 Cases 01–14 synthesis + external/legal anchor research

## 1. Rule

These clauses are deliberately written at the semantic level.

They are not:

- Constitution amendments;
- Domain Model amendments;
- database fields;
- enums;
- API contracts;
- RLS rules;
- UI states;
- automated decision algorithms.

Each candidate is classified by evidentiary status.

Classification:

- **EXTERNALLY SUPPORTED** — an authoritative external source supports the direction;
- **PRODUCT POLICY** — justified by ScoutSA's evidence methodology but not mandated by the cited law;
- **LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED** — implementation depends on jurisdiction-specific interpretation;
- **NOT YET EARNED** — insufficient evidence for ratification.

## 2. M1 — Epistemic Object Separation

### Candidate clause

ScoutSA must distinguish an actor's assertion from the evidence supporting it, any verification of it, any derived state based on it, and any later decision using that state.

Minimum conceptual chain:

Actor → Assertion → Evidence → Verification → Derived State → Decision

An assertion does not become truth merely because the actor is authenticated, verified, or institutionally affiliated.

**Classification:** PRODUCT POLICY / NOT YET EARNED

**Evidence:** Cases 1, 10 and 13.

**Re-witness targets:** Cases 1, 10, 13.

## 3. M2 — Scoped Authority and Conflict Preservation

### Candidate clause

Authority must be evaluated against the relevant subject, claim, scope, context and effective time. A role or relationship does not by itself establish universal authority.

When two legitimately authorised assertions are incompatible, ScoutSA must preserve the competing assertions and their provenance until an applicable authority rule or authorised resolution determines how the protected action may proceed.

Conflict is not resolution.

Resolution must not silently rewrite the original assertions.

**Classification:** EXTERNALLY SUPPORTED + LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

**External anchors:** Children's Act section 18 and section 28; FIFA safeguarding governance/roles.

**Evidence:** Cases 2, 8, 13.

**Re-witness targets:** Cases 2, 8, 13.

## 4. M3 — Purpose-Bound Authorization

### Candidate clause

Authorization is limited to the purpose, scope, context and time for which it is valid.

Authorization for one activity must not silently become authorization for another activity or disclosure purpose.

Candidate dimensions include opportunity participation, contact, media, publication, sharing and secondary use. The final taxonomy remains a product-policy decision subject to applicable law.

**Classification:** EXTERNALLY SUPPORTED + PRODUCT POLICY

**External anchors:** POPIA purpose specification and further-processing limitations; POPIA child-processing provisions.

**Evidence:** Cases 3, 6, 7, 8, 11.

**Re-witness targets:** Cases 3, 6, 7, 8, 11.

## 5. M4 — Temporal Applicability and Freshness

### Candidate clause

A historical fact that was valid or supported at one time must remain historically reconstructable but must not automatically be treated as sufficient for a later decision.

Current applicability must be evaluated against the relevant claim, purpose, scope, context, evidence freshness and authority state.

Authenticity does not by itself establish currentness, applicability or sufficiency.

**Classification:** EXTERNALLY SUPPORTED + PRODUCT POLICY

**External anchors:** POPIA information-quality requirement and purpose-dependent updating.

**Evidence:** Cases 3, 4, 5, 12.

**Re-witness targets:** Cases 4, 5, 12.

## 6. M5 — Derived-State Dependency and Invalidation

### Candidate clause

A derived state must remain explainable by the material inputs, rule/context and evaluation time that produced it.

If a material input is withdrawn, revoked, expires, becomes materially disputed or otherwise ceases to support the current evaluation, the historical derived result must remain reconstructable while its current applicability is reassessed.

A replacement input produces a new current evaluation; it does not retroactively rewrite the historical evaluation.

**Classification:** PRODUCT POLICY / NOT YET EARNED

**Evidence:** Cases 4, 5, 12, 14.

**Re-witness targets:** Cases 5, 14 and any case affected by the proposed dependency semantics.

## 7. M6 — Safeguarding Is Distinct From Verification

### Candidate clause

Verification of identity, role, organization or football claim must not by itself be treated as safeguarding clearance or authorization to contact, disclose or interact with a minor.

Safeguarding concerns require their own applicable control, reporting, resolution and audit pathway.

**Classification:** EXTERNALLY SUPPORTED + PRODUCT POLICY

**External anchors:** Constitution section 28; Children's Act; FIFA 2026 Safeguarding Policy and FIFA safeguarding guidance; SAFA academy regulations as historical institutional evidence.

**Evidence:** Cases 6 and 9.

**Re-witness targets:** Cases 6 and 9.

## 8. M7 — Disclosure and Republication Boundary

### Candidate clause

Information being discoverable, publicly available or present in an external record does not by itself establish that ScoutSA may reproduce, aggregate, transform, disclose or use it for a new purpose.

Any public-information pathway must still be evaluated against applicable purpose, further-processing, child-data, authority and other legal constraints.

**Classification:** EXTERNALLY SUPPORTED + LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

**External anchors:** POPIA purpose/further-processing provisions and child-processing provisions.

**Evidence:** Cases 7 and 11, supported by Case 12.

**Re-witness targets:** Cases 11 and 12.

## 9. Candidate invariant set

If later ratified, the minimum cross-domain invariant set would be:

1. `ASSERTION ≠ EVIDENCE`
2. `EVIDENCE ≠ VERIFICATION`
3. `VERIFICATION ≠ TRUTH`
4. `ROLE ≠ UNIVERSAL AUTHORITY`
5. `AUTHORITY ≠ CONSENT`
6. `PURPOSE A AUTHORIZATION ≠ PURPOSE B AUTHORIZATION`
7. `HISTORICAL VALIDITY ≠ CURRENT APPLICABILITY`
8. `AUTHENTIC ≠ CURRENT ≠ APPLICABLE ≠ SUFFICIENT`
9. `CONFLICT ≠ RESOLUTION`
10. `DERIVED STATE ≠ SOURCE OF TRUTH`
11. `VERIFICATION ≠ SAFEGUARDING CLEARANCE`
12. `DISCOVERABLE ≠ REPUBLISHABLE`
13. `INPUT WITHDRAWAL ≠ HISTORY ERASURE`

These are candidate invariants, not yet constitutional text.

## 10. Ratification discipline

No candidate above becomes authoritative merely because:

- it is logically attractive;
- it appears in multiple witnesses;
- it is consistent with a legal source;
- an investor would prefer certainty;
- implementation would be easier with a fixed rule.

Ratification requires:

Research → Evidence → Candidate clause → Adversarial re-witness → Gate decision → Ratification.
