# ScoutSA Constitution

## Who We Are

ScoutSA is a verified football opportunity network for South African grassroots football. We begin in the Vaal Triangle and expand outward.

We exist to make talent **visible and reachable**.

## Core Principles

### Principle 1 — Visibility with Integrity

Every player deserves the chance to be seen. Self-reported data alone is never enough. Verification is the foundation of trust.

### Principle 2 — Trust Over Growth

We will never sacrifice data integrity, guardian control, or player safety for user numbers or engagement metrics.

### Principle 3 — Guardian Control is Non-Negotiable

For every player under 18, the Guardian is a first-class stakeholder. Discovery, contact, and opportunities must respect applicable consent and safeguarding requirements.

### Principle 4 — Opportunity Must Be Feasible

Showing a player to a scout is incomplete if the player cannot realistically attend. Transport, guardian availability, medical-clearance status, consent, and academic context are core product concerns.

### Principle 5 — Verification Beats Self-Reporting

Coach, school, club, and future league verification create stronger trust signals than unsupported self-reporting. Verification status must always identify its source and scope.

### Principle 6 — Real-World Outcomes Over Platform Metrics

Success is measured by genuine football opportunities and sustained pathways, not profile views, downloads, or time spent in the app.

### Principle 7 — The Player Passport is the Source of Truth

The Passport is the persistent football identity of the player. Clubs, schools, and seasons can change; the identity record remains.

### Principle 8 — Discovery and Opportunity Layer, Not a Transfer Marketplace (v1)

Version 1 facilitates discovery and opportunity matching. Contracts, transfers, fees, and regulated negotiations remain outside the MVP boundary.

## Product North Star

Every Player Passport must answer two questions with increasing confidence:

1. Can this player play?
2. Can this player realistically access and succeed in the opportunity?

The first is **Football Readiness**. The second is **Opportunity Readiness**.

These are distinct. A player may be football-ready while not yet being opportunity-ready.

## Evidence Rule

> **Strength of claim must never exceed strength of evidence.**

No verification badge, readiness state, compliance claim, safety claim, or product metric should be presented as established unless the repository contains evidence sufficient to support it.

## Safety and Privacy Rule

ScoutSA handles sensitive contexts, including minors. Privacy, safeguarding, consent, data minimisation, access control, retention, and auditability are product requirements, not post-launch additions.

Legal compliance claims must be demonstrated against the implemented system and applicable law; documentation alone does not establish compliance.
