# ScoutSA Constitution

## Who We Are

ScoutSA is a verified football opportunity network for South African grassroots football. We begin in the Vaal Triangle and expand outward.

We exist to make talent **visible and reachable**.

## Core Principles

### Principle 1 — Visibility with Integrity

Every player deserves the chance to be seen. Self-reported data alone is never enough. Verification is the foundation of trust.

### Principle 2 — Trust Over Growth

We will never sacrifice data integrity, guardian control, or player safety for user numbers or engagement metrics.

### Principle 3 — Guardian Control is Non-Negotiable

For every player under 18, the Guardian is a first-class stakeholder. Discovery, contact, and opportunities must respect applicable consent and safeguarding requirements.

### Principle 4 — Opportunity Must Be Feasible

Showing a player to a scout is incomplete if the player cannot realistically attend. Transport, guardian availability, medical-clearance status, consent, and academic context are core product concerns.

### Principle 5 — Verification Beats Self-Reporting

Coach, school, club, and future league verification create stronger trust signals than unsupported self-reporting. Verification status must always identify its source and scope.

### Principle 6 — Real-World Outcomes Over Platform Metrics

Success is measured by genuine football opportunities and sustained pathways, not profile views, downloads, or time spent in the app.

### Principle 7 — The Player Passport is the Source of Truth

The Passport is the persistent football identity of the player. Clubs, schools, and seasons can change; the identity record remains.

### Principle 8 — Discovery and Opportunity Layer, Not a Transfer Marketplace (v1)

Version 1 facilitates discovery and opportunity matching. Contracts, transfers, fees, and regulated negotiations remain outside the MVP boundary.

## Product North Star

Every Player Passport must answer two questions with increasing confidence:

1. Can this player play?
2. Can this player realistically access and succeed in the opportunity?

The first is **Football Readiness**. The second is **Opportunity Readiness**.

These are distinct. A player may be football-ready while not yet being opportunity-ready.

## Evidence Rule

> **Strength of claim must never exceed strength of evidence.**

No verification badge, readiness state, compliance claim, safety claim, or product metric should be presented as established unless the repository contains evidence sufficient to support it.

## Safety and Privacy Rule

ScoutSA handles sensitive contexts, including minors. Privacy, safeguarding, consent, data minimisation, access control, retention, and auditability are product requirements, not post-launch additions.

Legal compliance claims must be demonstrated against the implemented system and applicable law; documentation alone does not establish compliance.



## Ratified Semantic Boundary — Gate 2 Assertion

Following the G2-SEAM-04 governance re-witness and explicit ratification on 2026-10-05, **Assertion** is a first-class semantic domain concept.

> **Assertion:** An attributable domain statement or claim about a defined subject, made or submitted by an actor within an applicable authority/scope where relevant, preserved with sufficient provenance and temporal context to distinguish the historical assertion from later applicability, and capable of being supported or evaluated by Evidence and Verification without itself constituting truth.

The following are ratified boundaries:

- ASSERTION ≠ EVIDENCE
- ASSERTION ≠ VERIFICATION
- ASSERTION ≠ TRUTH
- AUTHOR ≠ AUTHORITY
- EVIDENCE SUPPORTS ASSERTION ≠ EVIDENCE ESTABLISHES TRUTH
- CONTRADICTORY ASSERTIONS ≠ SILENT RESOLUTION
- HISTORICAL ASSERTION ≠ CURRENT APPLICABILITY
- ASSERTION VISIBILITY ≠ EVIDENCE VISIBILITY

This ratification does not establish universal authority, Guardian precedence, legal validity, truth-by-record, automatic verification, automatic conflict resolution, safeguarding clearance, public disclosure permission, AI adjudication, or implementation mechanics.


## Ratified Semantic Boundaries — Gate 1

The following semantic principles are ratified as governing constraints for the ScoutSA domain. They do not, by themselves, prescribe schema, RLS, API, UI, retention periods, freshness durations, or implementation mechanics.

### M1 — Epistemic Object Separation

Assertion, Evidence, Verification, Derived State, and Decision are distinct epistemic objects. No actor status silently converts one into another.

### M2 — Scoped Authority and Conflict Preservation

Authority is evaluated against the relevant subject, claim, scope, context, time, and applicable legal or policy basis. Incompatible legitimate assertions remain distinguishable until an authorized resolution process applies. No universal authority hierarchy is implied.

### M3 — Purpose-Bound Authorization

Authorization is limited by its defined purpose, scope, context, and applicable time. Authorization for one purpose does not silently authorize another purpose.

### M4 — Temporal Applicability

Historical validity does not by itself establish current applicability or sufficiency.

AUTHENTIC ≠ CURRENT ≠ APPLICABLE ≠ SUFFICIENT

### M5 — Derived-State Dependency

Derived state remains explainable through its material inputs, governing rule/context, and evaluation time. Material withdrawal, revocation, expiry, or dispute requires reassessment of current applicability without silently rewriting history.

### M6 — Safeguarding / Verification Separation

Verification of identity, role, organization, or football claims is not equivalent to safeguarding clearance or contact authorization. A safeguarding concern is not itself a finding of wrongdoing.

### M7 — Disclosure / Republication Boundary

Discoverability or public availability does not by itself establish permission to reproduce, aggregate, transform, disclose, or reuse information for another purpose.

### M8 — Child Participation / Voice

Where a matter materially concerns a child, the child's expressed view is preserved separately from Guardian authority and from the resulting decision. Where applicable law or policy requires participation or consideration of the child's views, that view must not be silently suppressed, overwritten, or equated with Guardian authorization or the final decision.

This principle does not create a universal child veto, Guardian override, age threshold, or capacity determination.

### M9 — Safeguarding Concern / Response Separation

Safeguarding concern/report, finding/substantiation, protective action, investigation outcome, and final resolution are distinct states/events and must retain provenance and responsible-authority context.

This principle does not create automatic guilt, exclusion, safeguarding clearance, or an invented investigation authority.

### Ratification Boundary

M1–M9 are semantic constraints, not legal advice or compliance certification. Applicable law, policy, competent authority, and product decisions determine context-specific operational effects.

No M1–M9 principle grants AI independent authority to determine truth, legal precedence, capacity, best interests, safeguarding guilt, or final resolution.

**Ratification record:** Gate 1 M1–M9 package accepted 2026-10-05 following the final re-witness and explicit ratification review.


## Ratified Semantic Boundary — Gate 2 Safeguarding Case/Response

Following the G2-SEAM-05 governance re-witness, ratification review, and explicit acceptance on 2026-10-06, **Safeguarding Case/Response** is a first-class semantic domain concept.

It represents the substantive safeguarding response context around a concern/report and, where applicable, subsequent finding/substantiation, protective action, investigation outcome, and final resolution. Not every case must pass through every state.

The following boundaries are ratified:

- CONCERN / REPORT ≠ FINDING / SUBSTANTIATION
- FINDING / SUBSTANTIATION ≠ PROTECTIVE ACTION
- PROTECTIVE ACTION ≠ INVESTIGATION OUTCOME
- INVESTIGATION OUTCOME ≠ FINAL RESOLUTION
- VERIFICATION ≠ SAFEGUARDING CLEARANCE
- PROTECTIVE ACTION ≠ GUILT
- CASE EXISTENCE ≠ TRUTH
- CASE EXISTENCE ≠ LEGAL VALIDITY
- HISTORICAL RESPONSE RECORD ≠ CURRENT APPLICABILITY
- SAFEGUARDING RECORD VISIBILITY ≠ PUBLIC DISCLOSURE
- AI ≠ SAFEGUARDING ADJUDICATOR

This ratification does not establish universal safeguarding authority, legal investigative/adjudicative authority, automatic exclusion, fixed investigation procedure, fixed retention duration, automatic public disclosure, compliance certification, or implementation mechanics.
